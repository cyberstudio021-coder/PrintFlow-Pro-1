Place your icon files here:
  - icon.ico  (Windows — 256x256, multi-size ICO)
  - icon.png  (512x512 PNG used by Electron at runtime)

You can generate a multi-size .ico from a PNG using:
  https://www.icoconverter.com
  or the 'sharp' npm package during your build pipeline.

The icon should use the PrintFlow Pro lime green (#B4FF39) accent on a dark background.
